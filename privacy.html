Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Puzzle Tech Studios LLC corporate website only. It does not describe the privacy practices of individual mobile applications.

Puzzle Tech Studios LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this informational website at puzdev.com and at any host address used to publish these same pages. We are an independent mobile software development company registered in Nevada, United States.

What this website is

This is a static corporate website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or other third-party tracking scripts. It does not offer accounts, checkouts, mailing-list signups, or a contact form that stores information on our servers.

We do not sell personal information collected through this website, and this website does not display advertisements.

Hosting and technical information

The site is published as static files. The provider that hosts those files may keep ordinary technical logs needed to operate and protect the service. Those logs can include details such as IP address, date and time, browser type, and the page requested. We do not control those logs, and this policy does not claim that the host stores no technical information.

When the site is served by GitHub Pages, GitHub describes its own handling of information in the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab).

Email

If you write to an address published on this site, such as hello@puzdev.com, you send us the information contained in that message, including your email address and whatever you choose to include in the body. We use that correspondence to read and respond to business inquiries, support requests, and related communications.

The provider that hosts the mailbox processes the message so it can be delivered. We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to handle the conversation and for ordinary business records. You may email us about a message you sent, including a request to delete that correspondence from our mailbox. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Links

Links to other websites, including app stores and product legal pages, leave this site. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

This website is a general business site. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information through forms, and it is not used to collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
