Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Puzzle Tech Studios LLC is an independent mobile app development company registered in Nevada, United States. PUZDEV is the public name of the company. This page describes the PUZDEV website only. It applies to puzdev.com and to any host address used to publish these same pages.

This website

This is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or other third-party scripts. It has no account, checkout, or contact form, and it does not ask you to create a profile.

We do not sell personal information, and this website does not show ads. It also does not operate a mailing list.

Hosting

The site is published as static files. The provider that hosts it may keep ordinary technical logs, such as IP address, date and time, browser type, and the page requested, in order to operate and protect the service. We do not control those logs. This policy does not claim that the host collects no technical information.

When the site is served by GitHub Pages, GitHub describes its own handling of information in the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab).

Email

If you write to hello@puzdev.com, you send us whatever you include in that message, such as your email address and the contents of the email. We use it to read and respond to business inquiries and support requests. We keep that correspondence for as long as needed to handle the conversation and for ordinary business records.

The provider that hosts the mailbox processes the message so it can be delivered. You may email us about a message you sent, including a request to delete that correspondence from our mailbox. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Our apps

God Focus: Bible Scroll and RoamXP are separate products. This page does not describe what those apps collect or store. When an app is available, its privacy disclosures are provided with the app and its store listing.

Links

A link to another website, such as an app store or GitHub, leaves this site. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Download links are added on our site only after the official listing address has been verified.

Children

This website is a general business site. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information, and it is not used to collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@puzdev.com.
